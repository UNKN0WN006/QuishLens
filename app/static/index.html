METHOD

One verdict, multiple independent signals.

A QR code is only a transport. QuishLens separates extraction, URL analysis, document-language analysis, ML, and threat intelligence so each part can be tested or replaced independently.

QR & document analysis

Images are decoded locally with OpenCV. PDFs are rasterized page-by-page so vector or embedded QR artwork can still be discovered, while extractable text supplies contextual evidence.

URL intelligence

QuishLens measures structural and lexical features such as URL length, subdomain depth, entropy, IP destinations, punycode, shorteners, encoding, and security-sensitive terms.

NLP/context layer

Nearby document language is checked for urgency, credential requests, payment pressure, fear cues, QR calls-to-action and named brands. These signals support—but never independently decide—the verdict.

Model & fallback

A trained scikit-learn model is loaded when available. Without it, a transparent heuristic fallback keeps the prototype operational and clearly labels the source of its score.

Threat intelligence

Known malicious URLs/domains can be loaded as local snapshots. The production-style workflow avoids browsing suspicious destinations during analysis.

Accessible output

Technical evidence is available for security users, while plain-language guidance avoids jargon and gives concrete next steps suitable for less technical adults or young users.
