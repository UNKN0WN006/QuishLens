UNDER THE HOOD
What happens after you upload a QR?

      

        
01
Decode only QR symbols
QuishLens cross-checks QR-only decoders (ZXing-C++, ZBar, and OpenCV) because stylised codes can defeat one decoder while another still succeeds. One-dimensional barcodes are deliberately ignored.

        
02
Understand the payload
Web links, EMV-style payments, UPI, Wi-Fi, contacts, SMS, phone numbers, authenticator secrets, locations, and plain text are handled differently.

        
03
Inspect the risky bits
URLs get domain/ML/threat analysis. Payment QR codes get TLV parsing, checksum verification, provider and receiver field inspection.

        
04
Explain before acting
The result shows what was decoded and why it matters. QuishLens does not intentionally open a suspicious destination.
